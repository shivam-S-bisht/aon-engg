HVAC

We provide energy efficient design services based on the latest technology oriented equipment and Indian/international standards. Our design is a methodical approach towards equipment sizing by taking into account various factors such as geography, climate, building configuration, orientation, space usage/load, zoning to optimize energy utilization. Our team is offering design solutions that comply to ISHRAE/ASHRAE, CIBSE guidelines, National Building Code (NBC) 2005, Local Building By-laws and Energy Conservation Act 2001and various standards like SMACNA, DW-144

ELECTRICAL

We provide design & consultancy services on optimum power requirement and energy saving which includes the following: UPS, PDUs, voltage stabilizers etc Computation of maximum power demand Sizing of Transformers, generators etc Power distribution systems Internal Lighting and Power distribution system Illumination design Earthing and lightening protection systems Voice and data networking systems.

PLUMBING

The main stress in PHE and plumbing has been put on water conservation and WHO standards. We design the PHE and plumbing services as per following: Manual on water supply and treatments published by Central Public Health and Environment Engineering Organization (Ministry of Urban Development, Govt. of India) Manual on sewerage and sewage treatment published by Central Public Health and Environment Engineering Organization National Building Code (NBC) 2005, SP-36 International Standards (ASPE/UPC/CIBSE) Regulations of the local authority.
